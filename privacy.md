# Palettes Privacy Policy

*Effective October 18, 2026*

**Palettes does not collect, sell or share your data.** There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We have no servers that receive your information.

## What stays on your Mac

Everything you copy and everything you create in Palettes — clipboard history, images, files you copied, bookmarks, tags, titles, checklists and settings — is stored only on your Mac, inside the app's private sandbox folder. It is not uploaded anywhere.

- **Encryption.** Your history is encrypted on disk. The key is created on your Mac and kept in your Mac's Keychain.
- **Recently Deleted** is off by default. If you turn on "Keep deleted items for 48 hours", deleted items stay on your Mac for 48 hours so you can restore them.
- **Backups** are off by default. If you turn them on, they are saved on your Mac only.
- **Export** creates a file in a place you choose. You can protect it with a password; without one, the file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe.
- **Web page sources** are off by default. If you turn on "Remember the web page each copy came from", the page address is saved with the item, on your Mac.
- **Passwords and sensitive items:** you decide in Settings › Privacy whether items that password managers mark as private are saved, deleted after a while, or never saved, and which apps Palettes should ignore.

## When Palettes connects to the internet

Palettes works offline. It connects to the internet only in these cases, and only because you asked it to:

- **Website icons (off by default).** If you turn on "Download the site's icon" for a bookmark that opens a website, Palettes downloads that site's icon from the site itself. Like any visit to a website, the site can see your IP address. No clipboard content is ever sent.
- **Links and actions you click.** Opening a link, an email address, a map, a phone number or a package tracking page hands it to your browser or to the right app. From that moment, that service's own privacy policy applies.

## Other apps on your Mac

- **Contacts and Calendar.** "Add to Contacts" and "Add to Calendar" create a file and open it in Contacts or Calendar, where you decide whether to add it. Palettes cannot read your contacts or calendars.
- **Share.** When you share an item, it goes only to the app or service you pick.
- **Apple Shortcuts.** Shortcuts you build can read items from Palettes and add to it. They run on your Mac, and what they do with the items is up to you.

## Permissions

All permissions are optional, and Palettes works without them.

- **Accessibility:** used only to paste the item you choose into the app you're typing in, by sending a single ⌘V. Palettes does not read your screen or other apps' content.
- **Touch ID / your Mac password:** if you turn on a lock, macOS checks your identity. Palettes never receives your fingerprint or password.
- **Files and folders:** Palettes opens only files you choose or copy yourself.
- **Open at login:** only if you turn it on.

Text and QR codes in images are recognized on your Mac, using Apple's built-in technology. Images are never uploaded.

## Purchases

If Palettes offers purchases, they are handled entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details.

## Children

Palettes does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data

Delete items or whole pages in Palettes at any time. To remove everything, quit Palettes, move it to the Trash, and also delete its folder in your Library's Containers folder.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
