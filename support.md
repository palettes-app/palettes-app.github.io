# Palettes Support

**Need help?** Write to **paletteclipboard@gmail.com**. We usually reply within a few days.

## Getting started

### How do I open Palettes?
Click the Palettes icon in the menu bar, or press the shortcut you chose when you set up Palettes. To change it, open Settings › General.

### Clicking an item copies it but doesn't paste it.
That's the default: click an item, then paste with ⌘V. To paste automatically:
1. Open Settings › General and turn on **Paste directly**.
2. In System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility, turn on Palettes.
3. Quit and reopen Palettes. macOS applies this permission only after a restart.

### How do I paste several items?
⌘-click the items you want, in order. Then choose **Paste Together** (or **Copy Together**) in the tray at the bottom, to paste them all at once. Or choose **One by One**: every ⌘V pastes the next item.

### Palettes doesn't open when I log in.
Turn on **Open Palettes when you log in** in Settings › General. If macOS asks, allow Palettes in System Settings › General › Login Items.

## Privacy

### Are my passwords saved?
You decide. In Settings › Privacy you can keep, auto-delete or never save items that password managers mark as private, and choose apps whose copies are never saved.

### Does Palettes send my data anywhere?
No. Everything stays on your Mac. See our [Privacy Policy](privacy).

## Your data

### I deleted something by mistake.
Use Undo right away. If **Keep deleted items for 48 hours** is on (Settings › History), open the trash button at the top of the page › Recently Deleted.

### How do I move my bookmarks to another Mac?
Settings › History › Move to another Mac exports your bookmarks and pinned items to a file. Open the same place on the other Mac to import it.

### Does Palettes sync between Macs or with iPhone?
Not today. Palettes keeps everything on this Mac.

## Requirements
macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
