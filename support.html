Getting started

How do I open Palettes?

Click the Palettes icon in the menu bar, or press the shortcut you chose when you set up Palettes. To change it, open Settings › General.

Clicking an item copies it but doesn't paste it.

That's the default: click an item, then paste with ⌘V. To paste automatically:

  1. Open Settings › General and turn on Paste directly.
  2. In System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility, turn on Palettes.
  3. Quit and reopen Palettes. macOS applies this permission only after a restart.

How do I paste several items?

⌘-click the items you want, in order. Then choose Paste Together (or Copy Together) in the tray at the bottom, to paste them all at once. Or choose One by One: every ⌘V pastes the next item.

Palettes doesn't open when I log in.

Turn on Open Palettes when you log in in Settings › General. If macOS asks, allow Palettes in System Settings › General › Login Items.

Privacy

Are my passwords saved?

By default, yes: they're saved and shown like other items. In Settings › Privacy › Passwords you can hide them, delete them after a while, or never save them, and choose apps whose copies are never saved.

Does Palettes send my data anywhere?

No. Everything stays on your Mac. See our Privacy Policy.

Your data

I deleted something by mistake.

Use Undo right away. If Keep deleted items for 48 hours is on (Settings › History), open the trash button at the top of the page › Recently Deleted.

How do I move my bookmarks to another Mac?

Settings › History › Move to another Mac exports your bookmarks and pinned items to a file. Open the same place on the other Mac to import it.

Does Palettes sync between Macs or with iPhone?

Not today. Palettes keeps everything on this Mac.

Palettes Pro

What's in Palettes Pro?

Pro adds Copy As and Reformat (including unit conversion), Automations, text in images (search, Select Text and Capture Table), the image editor, Paste Queue, multi-select with One by One and Copy Together, Float windows, and every banner, including your own photo.

Is there a free version?

Yes. Palettes is free to use, and Pro is optional. Without Pro you get clipboard history, search, copying a single line from any item, pages and bookmarks, pinning, privacy controls, backup and export, smart buttons for links, maps and phone numbers, tags and filters, the shelf, and Light or Dark with the default banner or a color. Nothing you saved is ever locked or deleted.

Can I try Pro first?

Yes. Choose the monthly or yearly plan to start a 14-day trial with every Pro feature. You're not charged until the trial ends. To stop, cancel in your App Store account settings at least 24 hours before it ends, and Palettes keeps working without Pro. The one-time purchase has no trial.

How much does Pro cost?

$2.99 a month, $19.99 a year, or $39.99 once. Prices are in USD; the App Store shows your local price before you buy.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel. On your Mac, open the App Store, click your name, then Account Settings › Subscriptions › Manage. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. A one-time purchase never renews.

Can I switch to the one-time purchase?

Yes. In Palettes, open Settings › Palettes Pro and choose Switch to Lifetime. Then cancel your subscription in your App Store account settings so it won't renew. The current period isn't refunded automatically; you can ask Apple for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep everything: your history, pages and saved items stay on your Mac, searchable and exportable. Pro features pause, and your Pro settings, like automations and your banner, come back when you subscribe again.

How do I get Pro on another Mac?

Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account. Then open Palettes Settings › Palettes Pro and click Restore next to Restore Purchases.

On how many Macs can I use Pro?

On every Mac signed in to the App Store with your Apple Account. Apple allows up to 5 computers per account for purchases (10 devices in all). If you reach the limit, you can remove an old Mac in your Apple Account settings.

Can my family use Pro?

Yes. Pro supports Family Sharing. In an Apple family group, you and up to 5 family members can use your subscription or one-time purchase, each with their own Apple Account.

Can my company buy Pro for its team?

Not in bulk yet. Your company can install Palettes on every Mac, but Apple doesn't sell in-app purchases through Apple Business Manager, so each person gets Pro with their own Apple Account. Need Pro for a team? Write to us.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
