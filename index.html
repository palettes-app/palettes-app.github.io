Do I need Pro?

If you copy and paste throughout the day, Pro can make a real difference, and you’ll appreciate it more with every shortcut.

You already get plenty without Pro: history, search, bookmark tabs, sections, checklists, pins, single-line copying, smart buttons, and privacy controls.

Pro helps you get things done faster. Fill out forms with a few ⌘Vs using Paste Queue. Keep instructions in view with Float. Extract tables from screenshots. Paste multiple items in the order you choose. And discover thoughtful little touches that make everyday work easier.

Small shortcuts. Time saved every week. All for about the price of a few cups of coffee a year.

Can I try Pro first?
Yes. The monthly and yearly plans start with 14 days of every Pro feature. You're not charged until the trial ends. To stop, cancel in your App Store account settings at least 24 hours before it ends. The lifetime purchase has no trial.
What happens if I cancel?
You keep everything. Your history, bookmarks and saved items stay on your Mac, searchable and exportable. Pro features pause, and your Pro settings come back if you subscribe again.
Does my clipboard leave my Mac?
No. Everything stays on your Mac, and Palettes collects no data. Purchases go through Apple. See the privacy policy for details.
Are my passwords saved?
By default they're saved like other items. In Settings › Privacy › Passwords you can hide them, delete them after a while, or never save them, and choose apps whose copies are never saved.
Does it sync with my other Macs or iPhone?
Not yet. Palettes currently keeps everything on this Mac, but we’re working on bringing the app to iPad and iPhone as well. For now, you can transfer your bookmarks and pinned items to another Mac using Settings › History › Move to another Mac.
Can my family use Pro?
Yes. Pro supports Family Sharing: you and up to 5 family members can use your subscription or lifetime purchase, each with their own Apple Account.
