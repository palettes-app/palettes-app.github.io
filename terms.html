Palettes Pro

  • Trial. The monthly and yearly subscriptions include a 14-day trial, once per Apple Account. When the trial ends, it becomes a paid subscription automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. If you cancel, Palettes keeps working without Pro. The one-time purchase has no trial.
  • Subscriptions. Pro is available as a monthly or a yearly subscription. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase, or when the trial ends if you started one. A subscription renews automatically for the same period unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the current period ends, and your account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your App Store account settings. If you cancel, Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for.
  • One-time purchase. A one-time purchase unlocks Pro with no renewals and no further charges. It has no trial.
  • Prices. The App Store shows the price in your local currency before you buy. Prices may differ by country or region. If a subscription price changes, Apple notifies you as its rules require, and you can cancel before the new price applies.

  • Payments and refunds. All payments are processed by Apple. Refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Another Mac. Use Restore Purchases in Palettes to bring Pro to another Mac signed in with the same Apple Account.

Without Pro

If you don't buy Pro, or your subscription ends, Palettes keeps working with its basic features. Everything you saved stays on your Mac and remains visible, searchable and exportable. Your Pro settings stay saved and come back if you subscribe again.
